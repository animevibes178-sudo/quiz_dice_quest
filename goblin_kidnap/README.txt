GOBLIN KIDNAPS PRINCESS - READY FOR GODOT 4
Same art as your image. Cut out, transparent background, upscaled 4x, sharpened and cleaned. Nothing redrawn.

HOW TO USE
1. Copy the "goblin_kidnap" folder into res://assets/ (you get res://assets/goblin_kidnap/...)
2. Project Settings > Rendering > Textures > Default Texture Filter = Nearest.
3. AnimatedSprite2D > Sprite Frames = sprites/<name>_spriteframes.tres, animation "default" (loops).

sprites/frames/      4 frames each, same-size transparent PNGs, feet aligned:
                     goblin_carry_princess_01-04, princess_crying_01-04, goblin_laughing_01-04
sprites/trimmed/     same frames cropped tight
sprites/<name>_spritesheet.png / _spriteframes.tres / _preview.gif
scene/               the big cutscene picture, label removed:
                     goblin_kidnap_scene.png (full, 2x) and goblin_kidnap_scene_1920x1080.png (center crop)
goblin_kidnap.json   frame sizes and file lists

NOTES
- The baked-in effects (tears, HA HA sparks) are part of the frames.
- The "OVERALL SCENE" label on the scene was painted out automatically; check the top-left corner.
